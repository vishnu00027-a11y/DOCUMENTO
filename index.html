Documento Privacy Policy

App: Documento
Developer: Shivank Tech
Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shivank Tech ("Shivank Tech", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Documento mobile application ("Documento" or the "App").

Documento is a document and productivity application that may provide features such as document viewing, PDF editing, document creation, scanning, OCR, image-to-PDF conversion, file management, notes, to-do lists, voice notes, and related productivity tools.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect and process only the information that is necessary to provide the features you choose to use.

1. Information You Provide

Documento allows you to create and work with your own content. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • PDF files and other documents.
  • Images and photos that you select or capture for document processing.
  • Scanned documents and OCR content.
  • Notes and to-do items that you create.
  • Voice recordings that you choose to create.
  • Other files or content that you intentionally import into the App.

This content is processed to provide the functionality you request, such as viewing, editing, converting, organizing, or exporting documents.

2. Photos, Media, and Files

Documento may request access to photos, media, or files on your device when you choose to import documents, select images, scan documents, create PDFs, or save exported files.

Documento does not need access to your personal files simply to allow you to browse or use the App. Permissions are requested when a feature requires them.

You can manage or revoke applicable permissions through your Android device settings. Some Documento features may not work correctly if the required permission is denied.

3. Camera and Document Scanning

Documento may request camera permission when you choose to use a scanning or camera-based feature.

Camera access is used to capture images or documents for features such as scanning and document creation. The camera is not intended to be accessed continuously in the background.

4. Microphone and Voice Notes

If you use Documento's voice recording functionality, the App may request access to your device microphone.

Audio is recorded only when you initiate a voice recording feature. The resulting recording is used to provide the voice-note functionality and is stored according to the App's storage behavior and the options available on your device.

5. Document and File Storage

Documento may store documents, notes, recordings, and other content locally on your device or in a location selected by you when you save or export content.

Unless a specific feature explicitly requires a network service, documents and other user-created content may remain on your device.

You are responsible for keeping backups of important documents, recordings, notes, and other content.

6. Data We May Collect Automatically

Depending on the services and SDKs enabled in a particular version of Documento, certain technical information may be processed automatically. This can include information such as:

  • Device model and operating system version.
  • App version and basic configuration information.
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information.
  • General usage and performance information.
  • Advertising-related identifiers where advertising services are enabled.

This information may be used to operate, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Documento.

7. Advertising

Documento may display advertisements through third-party advertising services. If advertising is enabled, advertising providers may process certain device, usage, or advertising-related information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Where required, Documento may provide consent or privacy choices related to personalized advertising.

8. Third-Party Services

Documento may use third-party services to provide certain functionality, including services related to advertising, analytics, crash reporting, payments, or other technical infrastructure.

When a third-party service processes information on behalf of Documento, that processing is governed by the applicable third party's privacy policy and terms.

Third-party providers may include services supplied through the Android ecosystem or other providers integrated into the App.

9. Payments and Subscriptions

Documento may offer optional paid features, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Payments made through Google Play are processed by Google according to Google's payment and privacy policies.

Shivank Tech does not require you to provide your complete payment card or banking credentials directly to Documento.

Information relating to a purchase, such as transaction status or subscription status, may be made available to the App so that paid features can be provided.

10. How We Use Information

Information processed through Documento may be used for the following purposes:

  • To provide the document and productivity features you request.
  • To import, edit, scan, convert, organize, and export content.
  • To save and retrieve your locally stored content.
  • To provide voice recording and related functionality.
  • To process purchases and subscriptions.
  • To display advertisements where applicable.
  • To diagnose crashes and technical problems.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the App.
  • To improve performance and user experience.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

11. Data Sharing

Shivank Tech does not sell your personal information.

We may share or allow access to information with service providers when this is necessary to operate Documento, provide advertising, process payments, diagnose technical problems, maintain security, or comply with legal requirements.

Documento does not give third parties ownership of your documents, photos, notes, or recordings merely because you use the App.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Content stored locally by Documento remains on your device until you delete it, remove the App, or otherwise manage the relevant files or data through your device. The exact behavior may depend on Android's storage and application-management system.

If information is processed by a third-party service, retention may be governed by that provider's policies and the purpose for which the information was processed.

13. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Documento. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should also use appropriate device security, such as a screen lock and current Android security updates, to protect documents and other personal content stored on your device.

14. Children's Privacy

Documento is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through Documento that should not have been collected, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

15. Your Privacy Choices

You can control several aspects of Documento's access to your device through Android settings, including access to files, photos, camera, and microphone.

You can also choose whether to use optional features that require particular permissions.

You may uninstall Documento at any time. Data stored locally by the App may be removed as part of the Android uninstall process, subject to Android's behavior and any files that you separately saved outside the App.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Documento's features, services, or legal requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
